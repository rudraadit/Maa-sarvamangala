Gateway to Vaikuntha
Sadhana, Swadhyaya, Sanyam, and Seva function as four interconnected pillars of practical spiritual growth, designed to refine the individual and elevate society. Together, they act like cultivating a sacred crop: Sanyam (self-restraint of senses, time, money, and thoughts) clears and protects the soil; Swadhyaya (deep self-introspection and study of uplifting literature) plants the seeds of divine wisdom; Sadhana (daily Gayatri mantra meditation) provides the spiritual nourishment to wash away old mental impurities; and Seva (selfless community service) is the ultimate blooming of this inner growth, treating the service of humanity as the literal worship of God.

Divine Preserver

Maa Gayatri

Maa Gayatri is the Mother of Wisdom. In the Gayatri Pariwar, she is not just a statue to worship, but the cosmic energy of good thoughts, right understanding, and pure intellect. Think of her as a divine light that cleanses your mind. When you pray to her or chant her mantra, you are asking for your thoughts to become positive, kind, and wise. Her grace belongs to everyone in the world, no matter who they are. By connecting with her energy, you replace negative, harmful thoughts with clear, loving wisdom, helping you become a better person and make the world a better place.

Maa Sarvamangala Ashrum -Solitude & Peace

Sarvamangala Ashram (also popularly known as Siddhashram Sarvamangala Dham or Maa Kali Dham) is indeed a highly prominent spiritual centre located at the holy Simaria Dham (Simaria Ghat) in the Begusarai district of Bihar, along the banks of the sacred Ganges River.


  • The Leadership: The ashram is founded and guided by the renowned saint Swami Chidatman Ji Maharaj  (Paramhans Karpatri Agnihotri). Under his leadership, it has become a central hub for spiritual activity in the Mithila region.
  • Core Spiritual Importance: It is designed as a Divya Shaktipeeth (divine centre of cosmic energy). The ashram features beautiful shrines dedicated to Maa Jagdamba (Goddess Durga) and Maa Kali, attracting politicians, dignitaries, and thousands of devotees seeking blessings.
  • The Centerpiece of Fairs: The ashram becomes the heart of devotion during the annual Rajkiya Kalpwas Mela (held during the Hindu month of Kartik) and the local Simaria Kumbh Mela gatherings. During these times, the ashram is continuously alive with classical Vedic chanting, grand bhajans, and spiritual discourses led by prominent gurus.
  • Social and Religious Value: Beyond its temples, the ashram is deeply valued by millions of followers as a place for serene meditation, holy dip coordination (Ganga Snaan), and community service programs like bhandaras (free community kitchens).

Real Voice

Gurudev:

"True discipleship is not merely bowing before the Master; it is the complete alignment of your character, speech, and action with the eternal truths of Dharma."

The sacred teachings of Paramhans Swami Chidatman Ji Maharaj remind us that the truest path to the Divine is paved with simplicity, humility, and a clean heart. Gurudev teaches that the most powerful prayer is the one hidden within selfless actions, where helping a person in need carries the same spiritual merit as worshipping in a temple. By anchoring our lives in quiet reflection and honoring our roots, we naturally rise above the daily chaos of the material world. True strength is found not in pride, but in the quiet, steady dedication to serving others with absolute purity and love along the holy banks of the Ganges
